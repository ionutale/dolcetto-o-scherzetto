# Dolcetto o Scherzetto

A cozy, endless Halloween night where two brothers walk one street collecting candy. This glossary fixes the game's own vocabulary — use these words in code, docs, UI copy and tests.

## Language

**Milo**: The 6-year-old brother; leads the duo, wears the wizard costume, braver during scares.
_Avoid_: brother one, player, character 1

**Tino**: The 4-year-old brother; wears the pumpkin costume, holds the bucket, hides his face during scares.
_Avoid_: brother two, sidekick

**The kids**: Milo and Tino together, always moving as a duo that shares one bag.
_Avoid_: characters, players, heroes

**Home**: A stopping point on the endless street; either a Treating Home or a Refusing Home (or a done one).
_Avoid_: house, building, door

**Treating Home**: A home that gives candy when knocked. 60% at launch; odds tune in BALANCE.md.

**Refusing Home**: A home that gives no candy; it is what unlocks the Scherzo.
_Avoid_: grumpy house, bad house

**Neighbor**: The person who opens a home's door; one of six archetypes; never mean, always funny.
_Avoid_: NPC, resident, owner

**Knock**: The player action at a home's door that starts the interaction.
_Avoid_: tap door, ring

**Treat**: One candy item; belongs to one of 10 visual types (which carry no value meaning).
_Avoid_: candy item, loot, pickup

**Candy value**: The 1–10 number rolled per treat when it drops; revealed as +N when collected. The sum of collected values is the bag.

**Bag**: The wallet — the running total of candy values; spent at the Bottega. There is one shared bag for the duo.
_Avoid_: inventory, wallet, money

**Record**: The biggest bag ever reached, saved across sessions; the only 'score'.

**Scherzo**: The action available at a Refusing Home — a harmless prank that makes candy burst out of the neighbor.
_Avoid_: prank, joke, trick (the noun is a different thing)

**Trick**: A purchasable scherzo move — its own staged show and gentle power tier; the free default is Boo!.
_Avoid_: power-up, upgrade, card

**Fan**: The row of owned tricks that opens when the Scherzo is triggered; the player picks one.
_Avoid_: menu, wheel, radial

**Bottega**: The witch's shop, opened by the on-screen button; prices are paid from the bag.
_Avoid_: store, shop (Bottega is the in-world name in both languages)

**Street**: The endless walkable world the kids roam; one axis, both directions.
_Avoid_: map, level, world

**Neighborhood**: Each stretch of ~10 homes with its own mini-theme (Leafy Lane, Foggy Bridge, Pumpkin Patch, Starry Alley) and slightly shifting odds.
_Avoid_: zone, area, chapter

**Neighborhood kids**: The 4–6 recycled costumed children roaming the street as ambience; they wave, never block, never take candy.
_Avoid_: NPCs, rivals, mob

**Ambience**: The per-neighborhood background bed (wind, leaves, drips…) plus sparse "sprinkle" one-shots (owl, creak, distant laugh).
_Avoid_: background music (music is a separate layer)

**Scary moment**: The rare scripted cameo (witch/werewolf/vampire crossing) with dimming and the kids' hug–tremble–giggle beat; always ends funny, never aimed at the player.
_Avoid_: jump scare, threat, attack

**Plush night**: The art style — storybook plush (rounded, soft shadows, no outlines) re-keyed to a deep indigo Halloween palette. Canonical: `ART-STYLE.md`.
_Avoid_: cartoon style, cute mode

**Done home**: A home that has already treated or been scherzod; it may re-gift at most once ever, then only tells a joke.
_Avoid_: used house, spent home
